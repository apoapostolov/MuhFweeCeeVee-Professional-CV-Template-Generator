## Entry Template

### YYYY-MM-DD - Short title

- **Symptom**: What went wrong on the surface.
- **Context**: Project type, task size, delegation pattern used.
- **Root cause**: Why it happened (be specific).
- **Failed attempts**: What was tried that didn't work.
- **Winning approach** (if found): What fixed or mitigated it.
- **Reusable rule**: The durable lesson or new default.
- **Related files**: Prompts, TODO entries, or other artifacts.
